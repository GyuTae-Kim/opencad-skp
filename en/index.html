OpenCAD SketchUp Viewer

Open .skp files saved in SketchUp 6~2026 in 3D in your browser without installation. You can turn tags on and off, view materials, groups and components, and measure the distance between two points.

The file is read entirely within your browser; it is not sent to a server. A modern browser that supports JavaScript and WebGL2 is required.
